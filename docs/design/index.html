Navigation

Desktop
Top bar: product and brand, the tagging and budget status strip, the signed-in person and role, Sign out. Side nav for the brand admin: Overview, Reviews, Themes, Outlets, Import, Digest; for an outlet manager: Overview, Reviews, Themes. At 768 the side nav becomes a row under the top bar.
Mobile
Bottom tab bar. Brand admin: Overview, Reviews, Themes, Import, More (Outlets, Weekly digest, Sign out). Outlet manager: Overview, Reviews, Themes. Detail pages (a review, outlets, digest) show a back link in the top bar.
Why
Overview, Reviews and Themes are daily destinations for both roles and sit one tap away; Outlets, Import and Digest are occasional admin tasks, grouped where a phone has no room for six tabs; managers never see controls they cannot use (Q-002). The urgent count on Overview opens the urgent-filtered list in one step (REQ-046).

Brief

Audience
A brand admin comparing every outlet of one brand at a desk, and outlet managers replying to their own outlet's reviews, sometimes on a phone. Reviews are in English, Devanagari Hindi and Hinglish.
Must feel
Premium and polished, like one product from a funded startup: deep navy navigation, a light blue-white canvas, white cards with soft shadows, electric blue pill buttons with a soft glow; still a clear information hierarchy with readable charts and tables (PRD L141 to L156).
Avoid
Clutter, decoration over review data, colour-only meaning, another product's logo, text or images.

Directions

  • Night launch (chosen) Round 2, product owner's direction with webinar.gg as the reference: Plus Jakarta Sans everywhere (Noto Sans Devanagari for Hindi), a #010D35 to #051B59 navy gradient for sign-in and navigation, #F8FAFF canvas, white cards at 20 px radius, #0B5CFF pill buttons with an rgba(59,130,246,0.3) glow, soft-blue #E6F0FE panels and badges, gradient words in titles, a blue heatmap ramp.
  • Instrument panel IBM Plex Sans Condensed, IBM Plex Sans, IBM Plex Sans Devanagari and IBM Plex Mono: one family for English and Hindi; cool graphite surfaces, ruled tables, tabular figures; one teal-blue accent for the primary action and current position; red kept for urgent, always with the word. Chosen in round 1, replaced in round 2 by the product owner.
  • Swiss grid Schibsted Grotesk with Noto Sans Devanagari; airy hairline grid. Lost: lower data density, and Hindi from a different family.
  • Ledger Spectral serif with Work Sans; ruled rows on grey paper. Lost: its one red accent competes with the urgent red.

Raised while designing

  • ambiguity No flows file exists (ux-flows not run); the screen inventory, states and navigation come from the stories, the PRD and the HLD. Navigation was proposed and confirmed by the product owner.
  • risk api/openapi.yaml does not exist yet; every error and status state is drawn from HLD sections 5 and 7 (401, 403, 409, 422, budget stop, OpenRouter 402, SMTP failure, 45 second draft deadline). openapi-spec must return these codes or the states change.
  • missing-screen US-02-002 (prompt versions), US-02-003 (replay tests), US-02-004 (classification evaluation), US-02-005 (tone check) and US-02-006 (seed) are command-line or developer work with no screen by design; their effects appear as the prompt version on drafts and the budget banner.
  • missing-screen US-01-010 tenant colours is a stretch feature and is not designed in the MVP round; tokens.css keeps colour in one place so a brand theme can be added later.
  • risk Fonts load from Google Fonts in these mockups only; the app must bundle Plus Jakarta Sans and Noto Sans Devanagari files, because the runtime allows nothing else (HLD section 3). Plus Jakarta Sans has no Devanagari, so Hindi always renders in Noto Sans Devanagari.
  • ambiguity Mark as replied is the approval (Q-008). The page tells the manager to post the text on the review site first; nothing checks that they did.
